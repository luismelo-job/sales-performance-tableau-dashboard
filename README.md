# Sales Performance & Profitability Analysis — Tableau

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes retail sales performance using **Tableau**, with a focus on identifying trends, profitability drivers, and differences across product categories, customer segments, and geographic regions.

The dashboard was designed from a **Business Analyst perspective**, translating raw transactional data into actionable business insights through KPIs, interactive visualizations, and filters.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The main objective is to answer questions such as:

- How are sales evolving over time?
- Which product categories generate the most sales and profit?
- Which sub-categories are the most and least profitable?
- Which customer segments contribute most to revenue?
- Where are sales geographically concentrated?
- Are there areas of the portfolio generating negative profit?

## 🧮 Calculated Fields

Several calculated fields were created in Tableau to support the analysis:

### Profit Margin %

```text
IF SUM([Sales]) = 0 THEN 0
ELSE SUM([Profit]) / SUM([Sales])
END
```

Other calculated fields include:

- Profit Status
- Average Order Value
- Discount Category
- Shipping Time (Days)
- Year-over-Year Sales Growth

These calculations transform raw transactional data into business-oriented metrics.

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

The analysis highlights several relevant business patterns:

- **Technology** is the strongest sales category and also generates the highest overall profit.
- **Consumer** is the largest customer segment by sales.
- Profitability varies considerably across sub-categories.
- Some sub-categories generate **negative profit**, indicating potential opportunities for pricing, discount, or cost optimization.
- Sales performance shows a strong upward trend toward the end of the analyzed period.
- Geographic performance is concentrated in specific U.S. states, with California representing one of the strongest markets.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, potential actions include:

1. **Prioritize high-performing categories and markets** when planning commercial strategies.
2. **Investigate loss-making sub-categories**, particularly those with consistently negative profit.
3. Review the relationship between **discount levels and profitability**.
4. Analyze high-sales / low-profit products to identify margin improvement opportunities.
5. Use geographic performance to support **regional sales and marketing decisions**.
